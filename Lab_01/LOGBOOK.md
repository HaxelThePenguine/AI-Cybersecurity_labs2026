**Hypotheses and rationales:** Hypothesis identifies an expectation in the existing text; Rationale explains why a comparison is informative. The latter is not a timestamp claim. Wrong original predictions remain visible. The revision register identifies the wording added during editing.

**Comparison reference:** IDs identify settings being contrasted, not warm-started weights or a claim of chronological ancestry. Each training starts afresh. **Threat model and metrics:** We prioritize coverage of rare PortScan activity and accept more alerts to investigate over overlooking this class. PS recall concerns correct PortScan identification; a PortScan predicted as another attack is a class FN but still raises an alarm. Attack FN counts attacks predicted Benign; Benign FP counts benign flows predicted as any attack. PS FP counts all non-PortScan flows predicted as PortScan. Selected references reflect task-specific validation tradeoffs; no numerical FN/FP cost ratio or false-alarm ceiling was set. Binary alarm metrics are reported in the extra test comparison, not used retrospectively to select these models.
